“Kommuna” App

A pet project about shared living for the creative class. It helps people find roommates by lifestyle, habits, and rhythm — not only by budget or neighborhood.

Role

Product designer

Focus

Research, UX, interfaces, web version, matching logic

Stage

Concept, prototype, user testing, ready-to-develop

Context

Shared rent is a common reality for students and young professionals, but finding “your” roommate is still almost impossible.

Most services stop at price and location. We wanted to account for real compatibility triggers: daily rhythm, communication boundaries, temperament, and even how tidy someone is.

My responsibility

  • Research into user pain points and habits
  • Brand and tone of voice for the project
  • Onboarding and matchmaking scenarios
  • Designing a flexible profile questionnaire
  • UX and visual layouts in Figma
  • Design system based on atomic components
  • OOD for preparing the product for development
  • Concept testing and feedback collection

I worked with Vitaly Gachkovsky as a creative pair — from idea to first prototype and user tests. We also worked on front and back for an MVP version of the product.

How I worked

  • Talked to potential users through quantitative and qualitative interviews
  • Mapped scenarios and pain points
  • Designed matching logic based on compatibility
  • Built an adaptive prototype and ran hallway tests

Outcomes

  • More relevant matches when looking for housing — users chose roommates by meaningful criteria, not at random
  • Easier decisions — a structured questionnaire plus a visual “spectrum” to assess compatibility faster
  • Less funnel friction — clearer questionnaire and onboarding reduced drop-offs
  • Validated the product hypothesis — positive feedback and confirmation that non-standard filters matter
  • Confirmed potential demand — hallway tests showed people felt they needed a service like this
