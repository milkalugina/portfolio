Rocketbank 2.0

A next-generation mobile bank focused on a transparent user experience and a strong digital identity.

Under NDA, this case is described without visual solutions. The focus is on problems, process, and product decisions.

Role

Product designer across 3 verticals

Period

August 2024 — August 2025

Stage

Pre-release, early MVP, active growth

Context

I joined the team early — while the product was still taking shape and many features existed only as sketches. It was an intense build-up: the team launched core banking scenarios and, at the same time, was forming how we work.

My scope covered key debit-card user flows: from card setup and management to cashback, referrals, and loyalty. I switched between tasks, shipped features from zero, and helped keep the visual and interaction system consistent.

My responsibility

  • Designing user scenarios for core banking features (card reissue and management, bonus programs)
  • Designing and evolving loyalty features: cashback, referral program, internal currency
  • Supporting a flexible design system for client interfaces
  • Owning design work from research to handoff, working with engineering, and documenting logic for them
  • Running design reviews and checking implementation quality through prototypes and user testing

I worked with PMs and analysts, took projects from idea to design and handoff, supported launches and internal MVPs, and gathered feedback from the team and early users.

How I worked

  • Competitive analysis and decomposition of user scenarios
  • End-to-end user flows, including rare and edge cases
  • Several concept iterations for discussion with product leadership
  • Product documentation and design specs for engineering
  • Collaboration with designers, analysts, PMs, engineers, and UX writers
  • Clarifying technical and legal constraints before finalizing decisions
  • Testing builds via APK and TestFlight

Outcomes

  • Prepared the product for scale — key user scenarios were worked through, edge cases covered, and design documentation ready for engineering and analytics
  • Aligned concepts and visual approaches — ran iteration rounds with the team and leadership to balance user needs and business constraints
  • Connected early users to the referral program — confirmed interest in the product already at an early stage
  • Launched a referral race with 3,000+ participants (pre-ordering started June 3) — validated the mechanic and early demand
  • Kept solutions consistent — evolved the internal design system so features looked and behaved as one experience
